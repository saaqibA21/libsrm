How to Check Your Books

1. Find your SRM Register Number (e.g. RA2611005010001) on your student ID card.
2. Type it into the search box above and click Check Loans.
3. You will immediately see which books you have borrowed and when they must be returned.

💡 Tip: Faculty and staff can enter their Employee ID number (e.g. 100278).
Library Loan Policy
  • Students: 15 days loan period
  • Faculty & Staff: 30 days loan period
  • Overdue Fine: ₹2.00 per day after due date
  • Books can be renewed if no other student has requested them
