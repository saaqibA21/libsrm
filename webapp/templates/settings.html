How to Activate Google SMTP (App Password):
  1. Sign in to your Google Account (myaccount.google.com/security).
  2. Make sure 2-Step Verification is switched ON.
  3. Go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  4. Type an app name, e.g. SRM Library, and click Create.
  5. Google generates a 16-character App Password (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop).
  6. Copy and paste that 16-character code into the App Password field above.
  7. Click Save Email Settings, then test it by clicking Send Test Email!
